Lint audio UK range

All Lint Audio products feature a lightweight and robust aluminium chassis with riveted corners, as well as preamplifier transformers encapsulated in steel for durability and noise reduction. Each unit includes CNC-machined end bells and is supplied with NOS (New Old Stock) valves.
Note: IEC mains cable, solder, assembly tools, and shipping are not included. Colours are selected upon application.
