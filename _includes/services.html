About us

Engineering Amplifiers for Home, Stage, and Studio.

PhonoVox

With 30 years of experience across the audio industry, within sales and product development, I have worked with British audio icons such as Naim audio and Bowers & Wilkins, championing adoption of ground-breaking designs like the Zeppelin in the mid-2000s.
I have gained a deep understanding of emerging trends and customers' audio experience. Being an audiophile myself, I appreciate and value quality engineering which is why I am honoured to be working with Lint Audio UK.

Lint Audio UK

Lint Audio has been manufacturing valve audio for the past 4 years in South Africa. Over three decades of experience in aircraft avionics and military design has allowed us to produce a range of competitive products.
With a focus on well-engineered, robust processes, valve audio need not be expensive, selecting valve types and materials that are easily sourced.
Our amplifier range is designed to be built in stages. Start with a pre amp. This may be used on existing solid state equipment. Then by internal bridges may be adjusted accordingly.
